📦
Inventory
The stock register that can't be fooled — what comes in, what goes out, always matching.

📥 Stock goes UP when…

  • Goods arrive from a supplier (GRN)
  • A cancelled sale returns items
  • Opening stock is loaded at go-live

📤 Stock goes DOWN when…

  • A sales dispatch leaves the store
  • Service uses spare parts on a job
  • Goods move to another warehouse
📖 Every single movement is written into a history book — who · what · when · why
🔖 Bonus: every unit carries its own serial number — like an ID card from purchase to customer. Selling the same unit twice is impossible.
